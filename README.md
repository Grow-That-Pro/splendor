# Splendor

Receive the true, the good and the beautiful, one Moment at a time.

Open Splendor, pick a Pack, and Moments play one after another: a Sound, an Image and a Passage together. Give it 10 minutes as a Session, or stay as long as you like. No accounts, no feed, nothing to scroll.

Anyone can curate a Pack and share it.
